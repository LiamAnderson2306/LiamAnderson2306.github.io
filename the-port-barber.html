Every port needs
a barber.

Perth's newest marina deserves better than a squeeze-in trim up the highway. The Port Barber is North Coogee's own — a proper chair, a sharp blade, good talk, and the Indian Ocean fifty metres from the door.

Hair and beauty, both sides of the shop, seven days a week. Drop the lines, take a seat.
