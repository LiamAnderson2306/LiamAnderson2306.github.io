The House

A hundred years
of good evenings.

Up the Great Eastern Highway, past where the city thins into jarrah, there is a house that has been welcoming people for more than a century. Two Sisters & A Chef is its latest chapter — a sensitively restored 1910s homestead of open fireplaces, antiques and quiet corners.

Five intimate dining rooms and an enclosed veranda, two sisters out front, and a chef at the pass cooking Australian and European food that changes with the seasons.
